Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Dayline is a productivity app for tasks, calendar, habits, focus sessions and notes. It runs on Android phones and on Macs, and is built to keep your data on your own devices. This policy explains what Dayline stores, where, and what you control.

The short version

  • Your tasks, habits, notes and settings are stored on your device.
  • Dayline has no servers of its own. The developer never receives your data.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no data selling.
  • If you turn on Google sync, a copy of your data is stored in your own Google Drive, in a private folder only Dayline can read.

Data stored on your device

Everything you create in Dayline (tasks, projects, habits, focus history, notes, reminders and settings) is saved in a private database on your phone. Images, drawings and voice recordings you add to notes are saved as files on your phone. Uninstalling Dayline removes all of this from the device.

Google sync (optional)

Google sync is off until you connect a Google account in Settings → Google sync. When you connect, Dayline asks Google for two permissions:

  • See, create and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive (drive.appdata). Dayline uses this to save one backup file of your Dayline data in Drive's hidden app folder. Dayline cannot see, open or change any of your other Drive files, and the file does not appear in your visible Drive.
  • Your email address. This is used only to show which account is connected.

The backup goes directly from your phone to your Google account, and from your Google account to your other devices where you connect the same account. It is never sent to the developer or anyone else.

Use of Google user data

Dayline's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from your Google account is used only to provide sync to you. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, it is not transferred to anyone, and no person reads it.

Device permissions

  • Notifications and exact alarms: to deliver task reminders and focus timer alerts on time.
  • Microphone: only when you record a voice note. Recordings stay on your device.
  • Internet: for Google sync, and to check GitHub for new versions of the app (no personal data is sent).
  • Vibration and starting at boot: for haptic feedback, and to restore your reminders after the phone restarts.

Dayline for Mac

On a Mac, your data is saved in a private file in your user account's Library folder (Application Support/Dayline). If you connect Google sync, Dayline asks for the same two permissions described above, and the sign-in tokens are kept in a file in that folder that only your user account can read. Notifications are used for task, habit and focus reminders. The Mac app checks GitHub for updates and downloads new versions from there. Deleting Dayline from Applications and removing the Dayline folder in Application Support removes everything from the Mac.

Deleting your data

  • Delete any item inside the app, or uninstall Dayline to remove everything from the device.
  • Turn off sync in Settings → Google sync → Disconnect.
  • Delete the backup in Drive: open Google Drive on the web → Settings → Manage apps → Dayline → Options → Delete hidden app data.
  • Remove Dayline's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

Dayline is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
